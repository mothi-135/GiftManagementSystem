# Gift Management System - Project Overview

## Introduction

The Gift Management System (GMS) is a web-based enterprise application designed to automate and manage the complete lifecycle of employee gift distribution programs within an organization.

The system enables HR teams, Facilities teams, Security personnel, and Administrators to manage gift events, identify eligible employees, collect employee preferences, generate QR-based gift coupons, distribute gifts, track inventory, and generate management reports.

The solution replaces manual gift distribution processes with a centralized, auditable, and role-based platform.

---

# Problem Statement

Organizations frequently conduct gift distribution programs during festivals, annual celebrations, employee recognition programs, milestone achievements, and corporate events.

Traditional manual processes create several challenges:

* Duplicate gift issuance
* Difficulty identifying eligible employees
* Inventory mismatches
* Manual reconciliation effort
* Lack of auditability
* Inaccurate reporting
* Poor visibility into gift distribution status

These issues result in operational inefficiencies and increased administrative effort.

---

# Business Objectives

The primary objectives of the system are:

1. Digitize the gift distribution process.
2. Eliminate duplicate gift issuance.
3. Automate employee enrolment workflows.
4. Enable QR-based gift validation and distribution.
5. Maintain accurate inventory records.
6. Improve visibility into gift event progress.
7. Provide real-time reporting and MIS.
8. Ensure complete auditability of critical operations.
9. Support role-based and location-based access control.
10. Reduce manual effort and operational overhead.

---

# High-Level Workflow

1. HR creates a Gift Event.
2. HR configures gift items and target employees.
3. Event is initiated.
4. Employees receive event notification.
5. Employees complete enrolment.
6. System generates QR codes.
7. Employees present QR codes during collection.
8. Issuer validates QR code.
9. Gift is issued.
10. Inventory is updated automatically.
11. Reports and audit logs are generated.
